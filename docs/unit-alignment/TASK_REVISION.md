# Rallye task revision

Implemented on 1 October 2026 in `app/journey/js/rallye-content.js`, following review of the three confirmed primary documents and coordination with the curriculum, assessment, language, source, engine, UX and teacher authors.

The revision keeps the six historical stops, all seven stable answer IDs, a 45-minute total and the enquiry into how imperial history helps explain plural British identities. It changes the response sequence from seven broadly similar history arguments to focused English products, including two analyses of authentic wording and one comment for a school magazine.

The revised engine contract distinguishes `expectedWords` (the suggested range) from permissive completion floors and maximum ceilings. Floors are 15/20/25/15/20/25/100; ceilings are 80/80/100/80/100/100/240. Formal completion does not establish quality. All substantive evaluation remains with the teacher.

## What changed educationally

The map and historical mechanisms remain central. The Company note explains how revenue could support power; Jamaica restores enslaved people’s agency; partition separates sovereignty from experience; Windrush connects empire to legal and social belonging; the Kenya statement shows that claims and responsibility can continue after independence. The final requires two developed connections, including resistance/independence and belonging/memory, rather than three compressed examples.

The final question is **“How far does Britain’s imperial past help explain British identities today?”** Its school-magazine audience makes register and explanatory clarity assessable. Students use explanatory relevance and representation to judge what their evidence can establish, consider another influence or complication, and qualify one source-based claim. A well-supported disagreement can earn full marks. This is not a generic debate about whether schools should teach history.

Victoria and Hague supply two required, locally available instances of actual language analysis. The source owner provides clearly attributed authentic quotations and separates them from editorial paraphrases. Students are not asked to infer original diction from summaries. The invented Jamaica label is explicitly labelled as a teaching example, not a real museum quotation. Canada and Hong Kong remain useful context; neither generates an additional required mini essay.

Each response has a visible operator and product. Optional help contains no more than two empty sentence frames and three short lexical glosses. These support an intellectual move without completing the historical argument. No vocabulary quota, compulsory personal disclosure, grammatical auto-grade or predetermined political conclusion is introduced.

The 40 marks are explicitly **local formative criteria requiring human feedback**. They cover accurate evidence, causal explanation, language/purpose analysis, audience and text type, coherence and precise intelligible English. The rubric does not convert automatically to official German or Abitur grades. Teacher examples are review material and are hidden from the normal pre-submission student view.

## Supplementary classroom materials

The six originally discovered Downloads documents included the now-confirmed primary handbook and detailed plans. The remaining concise plan and three DOCX files are supplementary. Their value is recorded in the specialist reviews, but none becomes a hidden prerequisite for this 45-minute activity.

`British Identities Today.docx` contains Fletcher’s comic contrast between an idealised British image and lived experience. `The_Bloody_British_Paul_Hawkins_Extract.docx` contains humorous islandness, identity labels and a closing image of an ended empire. These are claims and representations to analyse, not population facts. The vocabulary sheet offers transferable collocations but is not the full Mattinson article.

A teacher can use existing class copies in a later transfer: identify an author’s claim and test what a rallye case supports, qualifies or leaves untested. This does not add a compulsory eighth task or require copyrighted full texts in the app. Primary material mappings also permit a later teacher-supplied Tombs, Sanghera, Hirsch or Godden passage; the app makes no claim to reproduce those materials.

## Validation

All seven models fit their suggested ranges: 28, 25, 44, 26, 38, 43 and 147 words by whitespace count. Stage time totals 45; each station rubric totals five and the final totals ten. All existing source and territory IDs were retained. The source owner’s authentic quotation additions and partition/Windrush provenance corrections have landed. A module validation confirms required metadata, source resolution, bounded supports, authentic excerpts at both analysis stops, timing, marks and model lengths.

The independent assessment reviewer accepted the final content after three precision fixes: abolition taking effect in 1834; the partition testimony’s earlier displacement distinguished from a consequence of the 1947 event; and regret distinguished from an admission of legal liability in the Kenya statement. Engine migration/export tests and responsive UI inspection are coordinated with their respective owners. Their reports provide the integration evidence; this authoring report does not substitute a self-review for those checks.
