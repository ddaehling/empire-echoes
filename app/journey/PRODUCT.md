# Empire / Echoes — product brief

Register: product with an expressive, map-led educational identity.

Q2 English students in Schleswig-Holstein, working at grundlegendes Niveau (B2), use laptops and tablets in a bright classroom to investigate how imperial history shapes present-day British identities. Combine a generous, tactile historical atlas, territory stories and an editable evidence enquiry with a final written English comment. Identity is plural and contested: distinguish England, Britain, the UK and their peoples; avoid claims about a single national psychology.

The new visual identity must visibly depart from the earlier green/coral site. Review both user-supplied Sonnet/Opus HTML galleries. Use locally bundled fonts, strong hierarchy, comfortable reading, accessible controls and a distinctive map stage. Genuine spatial unfolding, territorial-change feedback and fullscreen support are core requirements. Reduced motion and WebGL fallback remain usable.

Opening a territory focuses the existing global globe or flat map on that place, highlights it and fades other geography and the surrounding app. Keep the active story, photographs and sources readable, preserve projection/year/camera when returning, and support deep links, keyboard focus and reduced motion. Curated photographs have accurate captions and provenance.

Give each of the six historical stops a readable introduction explaining unfamiliar people, events and concepts. Present essential evidence and one concrete task with one editable response. Optional language help, notes and extended research stay in disclosures. Remove score displays, rubrics, response-length advice, counters and completion gates. The final school-magazine comment connects historical evidence with plural identities. Prepared local sources suffice; authentic extracts, summaries and the invented teaching label are distinguished.

Support saved progress, map excursions, optional evidence notes, review and portable downloads. Learners mark their own progress and can edit after review. Changing prompts preserves earlier work with its original questions in separate recovery files. Provide a matched teacher guide with discussion examples, unit/material connections and a printable handout. This activity practises selected English skills within the wider unit; it does not replace the semester's listening, speaking, mediation or literature work.

Keep /app/ and /app/next/ unchanged. New work lives under /app/journey/ except tests, documentation and package scripts. Use separate local storage. The first redesign was completed within its ten-hour deadline. The current source hierarchy, ownership and evidence gates are in docs/learning-revision/CONTRACT.md and docs/learning-revision/ACCEPTANCE.md.
