The colour is only a beginning.

“British” could mean direct government, a protectorate, a company or a self-governing dominion. Change the colour view and select a place to see the distinction.

Hatched areas show partial coverage. Borders are approximate; each snapshot shows the end of the selected year. Uncoloured land was never empty or without a history.

A globe avoids the distortions of a flat projection, but neither view shows consent, lived experience or informal influence. Use the sources to ask whose perspective is missing.
