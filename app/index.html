A map is a starting point.

Colour shows places under a form of British authority during the selected year. A single colour includes very different arrangements: direct rule, indirect rule and limited authority. Choose Types of rule to compare these simplified levels of authority. Select a place for its specific form of government. Hatched areas represent partial coverage.

Boundaries are approximate and use modern geographic units. Changes within a year are simplified; a place can remain coloured in its year of independence. The map does not measure informal influence, people’s experiences, or the human cost of empire.
