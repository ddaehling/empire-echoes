## The revised student route

| Stop                    | Product                                                                    |   Time | Suggested words |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -----: | --------------: |
| Profit and power        | Explain how revenue supported Company power                                |  4 min |           20–35 |
| Freedom and memory      | Rewrite an invented museum label for teenage visitors                      |  5 min |           20–35 |
| Rule and resistance     | Analyse wording from Victoria's 1858 proclamation                          |  6 min |           35–50 |
| Departure and division  | Outline political change and a source-supported human experience           |  4 min |           20–30 |
| Migration and belonging | Explain legal status versus accepted belonging                             |  5 min |           25–40 |
| Remembering empire      | Analyse wording in the 2013 Kenya statement                                |  6 min |           35–50 |
| Whose Britain?          | Comment on how far imperial history helps explain British identities today | 15 min |         120–180 |

The final comment requires two developed historical-to-present connections, a complication and a source limit. Its 15 minutes allocate 2 to planning, 9 to writing, 3 to revision and 1 to export. The teacher's 60-minute wrapper adds 5 minutes of orientation before the activity, then 7 minutes of paired oral defence and 3 minutes for a revision target.

Comparable unique core reading fell from **2,475 to 1,999 words (19.2%)**. Suggested writing fell from **410–590 to 275–420 words**. There are eight core source cards and seven optional cards. The exact task is visible before reading; optional sentence frames and vocabulary help remain beside the writing area. Core materials work locally and external research is optional. **45 minutes remains a planning estimate, not a pupil-tested duration.**

Original excerpts, editorial summaries and the invented museum label are explicitly distinguished. Source checks corrected the proclamation provenance, Windrush page locators and the partition letter's 1946 date. The Kenya task distinguishes regret from legal liability. Models, prompts and rubrics are shared with the teacher key; all 40 marks require human judgement. The revised PDF handout has 24 pages, visually checked throughout.

## Saved work and map focus

The content revision is `q2-english-2026-10-01`. Earlier work is preserved with its original questions, student identity and downloads. Revised assignments start separate answers; earlier completed work is never silently regraded against the new questions. Storage-failure recovery is tested.

Opening a territory moves the existing global scene into an accessible focus dialog. The original globe canvas or fallback SVG zooms and highlights the place while surrounding geography and the rest of the app fade. Story text, photographs and evidence remain readable. Close, Escape and browser navigation restore the atlas state and keyboard focus; rallye drafts survive excursions. Small territories receive a locator within regional context. Runtime WebGL loss switches to a usable SVG map.

The flat world's fixed 180° seam still divides date-line territories such as Fiji; a visible caption explains this and how to see the opposite side. The globe gives the continuous view. Modern geographic approximations and year-end snapshots remain disclosed.
