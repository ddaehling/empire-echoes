# Empire / Echoes — product brief

Register: product with an expressive, map-led educational identity.

Q2 English students in Schleswig-Holstein, working at grundlegendes Niveau (B2), use laptops and tablets in a bright classroom to investigate how imperial history shapes present-day British identities. Combine a generous, tactile historical atlas, territory stories and a 45-minute evidence rallye with a final written English comment. Identity is plural and contested: distinguish England, Britain, the UK and their peoples; avoid claims about a single national psychology.

The new visual identity must visibly depart from the earlier green/coral site. Review both user-supplied Sonnet/Opus HTML galleries. Use locally bundled fonts, strong hierarchy, comfortable reading, accessible controls and a distinctive map stage. Genuine spatial unfolding, territorial-change feedback and fullscreen support are core requirements. Reduced motion and WebGL fallback remain usable.

Opening a territory focuses the existing global globe or flat map on that place, highlights it and fades other geography and the surrounding app. Keep the active story, photographs and sources readable, preserve projection/year/camera when returning, and support deep links, keyboard focus and reduced motion. Curated photographs have accurate captions and provenance.

Plan 30 minutes for six focused notes, explanations, audience-aware rewriting and analysis of authentic wording, followed by 15 minutes for planning, writing, revising and handing in a final comment. Required responses examine evidence, competing interpretations, colonial agency, migration and modern identities. State the operator and intended product before reading; offer small optional language supports. Distinguish authentic wording, editorial summary and invented teaching stimulus. Curated local sources must suffice for the timed route; extended research is optional.

Support saved progress, map excursions, citations, review, explicit completion and portable reports. Changing prompts must preserve earlier work with its original questions and prevent silent regrading. Written work requires teacher judgement; the formative rubric is not an Abitur grade. Provide a substantive, precisely matched teacher key, unit/material connections and a printable handout. This activity practises selected English skills within the wider unit; it does not replace the semester's listening, speaking, mediation or literature work.

Keep /app/ and /app/next/ unchanged. New work lives under /app/journey/ except tests, documentation and package scripts. Use separate local storage. The first redesign was completed within its ten-hour deadline. The current source hierarchy, ownership and evidence gates are in docs/unit-alignment/WORKING_CONTRACT.md and docs/unit-alignment/ACCEPTANCE.md.
