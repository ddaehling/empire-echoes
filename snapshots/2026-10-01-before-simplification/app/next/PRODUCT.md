# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Secondary-school history students completing an independent British Empire enquiry and assessment on laptops, tablets or phones. The existing classroom version at `/app/` remains available for the user's lesson.

## Purpose

Explore the geography and changing forms of imperial power, then complete a substantive assessment using several kinds of historical thinking. Every assignment requires a response before an attempt can be submitted. Objective marking is separate from human review of written arguments.

## Personality

Curious, precise, confident. Preserve the atlas's locally bundled serif and humanist sans typography and green/coral identity, with a more dimensional spatial experience and clear product controls.

## Design principles

1. Two destinations: Explore and Assessment. No guided lessons or teacher section in this version.
2. A genuine, interactive data-driven globe earns WebGL; a flat atlas remains available for comparison and fallback.
3. Short context, full evidence on request, purposeful motion with reduced-motion alternatives.
4. Completion is observable: eight answered tasks, explicit submission, saved progress and a portable report.
5. Preserve every pre-existing app file and the old live URL. Use a separate route and separate local storage.

## Accessibility

Keyboard alternatives for spatial and ordering tasks, readable contrast, semantic forms, explicit errors, reduced motion, WebGL fallback and responsive layouts. Local-first, with no claims of server submission or secure exam proctoring.
